    Our story

    It started with Danny

    Dellis began with Danny, a young boy in elementary school who loves writing books, making pictures and building STEM projects. His imagination inspired a place where kids' creativity can come to life.

    We believe in a world where everyone feels accepted and valued. Our books, songs and products celebrate what makes each of us different, and every purchase helps young kids keep dreaming, creating and imagining.

    Empowering minds, celebrating differences.
    Questions

    Good to know

    How long does shipping take?

    Our gear is made especially for you when you order, so timing depends on the item. You'll see the estimate at checkout on the store you buy from.

    Where do I pay, and who ships my order?

    Books are sold through Amazon and Barnes & Noble. Clothing and gear are sold through our Printful and Printify stores, which print, pack and ship your order and send you tracking.

    If I buy several items, will they arrive together?

    Items from the same store may still ship separately because they're printed in different places. Items from different stores always ship separately.

    Can I return or exchange something?

    Returns follow the policy of the store you bought from, linked at the bottom of each store page. If something isn't right, email us and we'll help sort it out.
