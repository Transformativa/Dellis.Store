The short version

World Facts is an educational app for children. It does not require accounts or ask children for their names, email addresses, birthdays, photographs, precise locations, or messages. The app has no advertising, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics. It stores certain preferences on the device. To download educational content, it contacts GitHub Pages, which may process IP addresses and basic request information. Purchases are not currently enabled.

Who we are

World Facts is developed and published by Transformativa LLC under its Dellis brand. Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@gettransformativa.com.

Information you provide

World Facts has no registration, login, chat, comments, or user-upload feature. We do not ask children or parents to submit personal information through the app.

Information stored on your device

The app stores favorites, the number of free content views used, the selected text size, and cached educational facts locally on the phone or tablet. This information is not transmitted to Transformativa LLC by the app. Removing the app generally removes its local app data, subject to device backup and operating-system behavior.

Internet connections and hosting

When launched, World Facts downloads educational data files from transformativa.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages. These requests necessarily expose the device's IP address and basic request information, such as request time, to the hosting service. GitHub may process or log that information under its own practices. The app does not send an account identifier, a device ID, or user-created content in those requests. In the current app implementation, GitHub Pages is the only service the app directly contacts to obtain content; this description excludes operating-system and app-store communications outside the app's control.

Educational information sources

The educational facts are compiled from public sources including World Bank Open Data, Wikidata, Unicode CLDR, Glottolog, and Pew Research Center. The app identifies its data sources. These sources do not receive information directly from the app merely because their data is used.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are not currently switched on. The app may display Subscribe and Restore options, but the purchase integration is a placeholder and does not process transactions. When subscriptions are enabled, we will update this policy before release to explain the payment platforms, purchase-service provider, and any identifiers or purchase data processed. Apple or Google may independently process app-store purchases and diagnostics according to their own privacy policies.

External links and parental controls

The app's only external website link is the Dellis store logo, which opens dellis.store after a parental gate. Subscribe and Restore controls are also behind a parental gate. The gate has been tested in the web version; the iPhone keypad implementation still requires testing on a physical device before release. Parents should supervise children's use of external websites.

Children's privacy

World Facts is intended for children. In the current implementation, the app does not contain ad networks, behavioral tracking, third-party analytics, or a feature for children to submit personal information. Technical request data may nonetheless be processed by GitHub Pages as described above. We will review the final app and store disclosures against applicable children's privacy requirements before publication.

Analytics and diagnostics

The current app does not include a dedicated analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking SDK. Apple and Google may independently collect crash and diagnostic information through their operating systems and developer consoles, subject to their own practices. Transformativa LLC may be able to view reports made available by those platforms.

Sharing and selling information

We do not sell children's personal information or share it for targeted advertising. The hosting provider may receive technical request information to deliver app content, as described above. We do not build behavioral profiles of children.

Security

The app downloads its content over HTTPS. We aim to minimize the information handled by the app and use platform and hosting protections appropriate to the services involved. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You can remove locally stored app information by deleting the app, subject to device backup settings. For questions or privacy requests, contact contact@gettransformativa.com. Where information is processed independently by Apple, Google, or GitHub, their privacy controls and policies may also apply.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as the app evolves. We will publish the updated policy at its public URL and change the effective date. Where applicable law requires parental notice or consent for a new practice, we will obtain it before implementing that practice.
